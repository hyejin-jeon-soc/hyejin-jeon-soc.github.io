Research Experience
======
* Research Assistant for Dr. Long Doan, University of Maryland, Department of Sociology (May 2025–Present)
  * Project: "Public Attitudes toward State and Corporate Privacy Invasions." Analyzed open-ended survey responses on privacy using topic modeling; designed a coding scheme for privacy concerns and applied a large language model (LLM) to classify open-ended responses into concern categories across two survey waves.
* Internship, Maryland Department of Health, CHPHS, Program Compliance Unit (May 2024–Aug 2024)
  * Project: "Quality Improvement and Data Aggregation Project." Developed and implemented a centralized data platform to consolidate quarterly budget expenditure reports from local HIV service offices throughout Maryland; replaced an email-based, multi-step correspondence process with a Smartsheet form submission workflow.
* Research Assistant for Dr. Julia Chuang, University of Maryland, Department of Sociology (Aug 2022–May 2023)
  * Project: "Brokered Passport." Attended expos in Seoul and Vietnam to recruit participants (EB-5 immigration brokers); organized and conducted in-depth interviews; managed the data repository; reviewed interview transcriptions to define qualitative codes (NVivo).
* Research Assistant for Dr. Reeve Vanneman, University of Maryland, Department of Sociology (May 2020–Aug 2020)
  * Project: "Text Mining to Analyze Cultural Changes about Motherhood." Modified the existing gendered name dictionary to estimate gender for Korean names using the Python package gender_guesser; preprocessed Korean movie plot data translated into English.
* Visiting Researcher, Korea Local Information Research & Development Institute, Seoul, Korea (Jan 2018–Jun 2018)
  * Project: "Information Network Village." Constructed a dataset on village infrastructure, internet accessibility, and educational facilities; calculated informatization level scores for South Korean rural areas.
* Visiting Researcher, Korean Women's Development Institute (KWDI), Seoul, Korea (May 2016–Jun 2017)
  * Research projects on child abuse prevention campaigns and women's lifecycle safety policy; conducted literature reviews, data analysis, and focus groups.

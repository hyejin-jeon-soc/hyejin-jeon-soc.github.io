Instructor of record, Summer 2025.

This course is designed to guide students through the logic, methods, and ethics of sociological research. It explores why systematic research is essential for understanding the social world and introduces both qualitative and quantitative research techniques. Topics include the relationship between theory and data, research design, measurement, sampling, surveys, experiments, and qualitative fieldwork.

The course emphasizes critical evaluation of scientific findings and teaches students how to assess the reliability and limitations of research encountered in both academic and everyday settings. As part of the lab component, students gain hands-on experience using statistical software to analyze and interpret quantitative data. By the end of the course, students will understand how sociological knowledge is produced and develop the tools to become more critical and informed consumers of social research.
